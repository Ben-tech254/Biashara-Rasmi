Grants · Wider markets
When I registered my business, I got grants that enabled me to buy my machines.

Mama Peres

Joopers Busy Ltd

Turns locally grown hibiscus and sugarcane into MitMit products. Registering opened two doors for her:

  • Access to grants: money to buy her machines.
  • Access to wider markets: supplying supermarkets.
